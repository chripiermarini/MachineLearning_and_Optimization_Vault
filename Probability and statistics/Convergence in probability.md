Convergence in probability is one of the most important and useful concepts to understand if, by collecting an increasing amount of samples from a population, the estimate of the parameter of a specific model becomes 'reliable' and moves more towards the true value of the parameter.

For the specific case of the *convergence in probability*, the mathematical condition that holds is the following:

$$
\lim_{n\rightarrow \infty}P( |\hat{\theta}_n - \theta| > \epsilon) = 0
$$
