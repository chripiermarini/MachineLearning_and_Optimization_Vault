In this document we define the theory of *convex sets*. 

##### Line segments

Taken two points $x_1$ and $x_2$ distinct, we have

$$
y = \theta x_1 +(1-\theta)x_2
$$
where $\theta \in \mathbb{R}$. $y$ forms the *line* passing through x1 and x2. $\theta= 0$ equals to $x_2$ and $\theta = 1$ equals $x_2$. The set of points between $x_1$ and $x_2$ (computed by selecting values of $\theta$ between 0 and 1) define the *line segment*.

hence, the *line* is infinite while the *line segment* is not. 

A set $C \subseteq \mathbb{R}$ is defined *affine* if the line through two distinct points in $C$ still lies in $C$.
