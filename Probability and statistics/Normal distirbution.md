*Normal Gaussian distribution*: this distribution starts from the simple univariate Normal Gaussian distribution:

$$

\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi \sigma^{2}}} exp\left({-\frac{(x-\mu)^{2}}{2\sigma^2}}\right)

$$
